Prices: Mercari Japan and eBay show the rolling 3-month average sold price; MSRP is the original retail. Use the currency picker (top right) to switch USD / JPY / EUR / GBP / AUD. Click 🔍 next to a price to open a fresh sold-listings search on that marketplace.

Prize Filter: LOP shows only Last-One-Prize figures (the consolation prize for the last sealed ticket); Grail shows only the rare-value figures. Toggling both shows the union.

Set pages: click any Kuji set name to open a focused page with all its prizes plus an EV-per-ticket calculator comparing average secondary value to average MSRP.

Backups: saved Wishlist and Collection data lives in this browser only. Use ↓ Export to back it up as a JSON file; ↑ Import to restore on another browser.

Mercari Japan and eBay prices are read-only snapshots of recent sold-listing averages, not live data. BBTS cells link to the live product page when the figure is currently listed and otherwise show MSRP as plain text; cells without a recorded MSRP are left blank. A small tribute

This price tracker is built by a Dragon Ball collector for Dragon Ball collectors. Every figure here is a recreation of one of Akira Toriyama's panels — without his decades of work there's no kuji to chase, no grail to grade, no ★ to mark on a wishlist. The art scattered across this site is a small bow to that.
