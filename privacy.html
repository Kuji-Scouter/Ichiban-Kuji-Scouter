Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-19

Ichiban Kuji Scouter is a static, fan-made hobby site. It does not require an account, does not collect personal information directly, and does not run server-side code. This page describes the limited data that is incidentally collected when you visit, and where it goes.

Hosting and access logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Fastly's content-delivery network. GitHub and Fastly receive and may log standard request data when you load any page on this site, including your IP address, user agent, referring URL, and the time of the request. This logging is controlled by GitHub and Fastly, not by this site. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Local storage in your browser

If you use the Wishlist or Collection features, your saved entries are kept in your browser's local storage on your device only. They are not transmitted to this site, GitHub, or any third party. Clearing your browser storage (or using the ↓ Export button to back up to a JSON file) is fully under your control.

Outbound links and affiliate cookies

This site links out to third-party marketplaces (Big Bad Toy Store, Amazon, eBay, Mercari, ZenMarket, Buyee, Jump Ichiban, Bait Me, MyFigureCollection, db-kuji.com, kuji-collection.com). Once you click through, those sites operate under their own privacy policies and may set their own cookies or tracking identifiers. Affiliate links to Big Bad Toy Store may attach a tracking parameter that lets BBTS attribute a purchase to MatchesPeaches; see the disclaimer page for more on affiliate links.

Analytics and advertising

This site does not currently run any first-party analytics, advertising, or tracking script. If that ever changes, this page will be updated to disclose what is added.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

Sale of data

The site does not sell, rent, or trade visitor data — it has none to sell.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change to reflect that. Material changes will also be noted at the top of this page.
